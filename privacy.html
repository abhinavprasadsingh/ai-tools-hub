Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

AI Tools Hub respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens when you visit and use this website.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of AI Tools Hub does not require an account or registration. The tools process the text you enter directly in your browser using JavaScript. The current tool functions do not send your entered text to an AI API or store it in a website database.

Your hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP address, browser information and server request logs, to deliver and secure the website.

2. How the Tools Work

The current tools use basic browser-based text-processing logic. They do not connect to an external generative AI service. Results may be incomplete or inaccurate, so please review them before use.

3. Cookies and Analytics

The current version of this website does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies. This may change if analytics, advertising or other third-party services are added in the future. This policy will need to be updated if that happens.

4. Third-Party Services

The website currently loads fonts through Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Please review Google's privacy information for details about its processing.

The website's hosting provider may also process technical data necessary to serve the website.

5. Contact Form

The current contact form is a front-end interface only. It is not connected to an email service or database, and submitting it does not currently send us a message.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not submit sensitive personal information into the tools.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when website features or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the contact details published on our website once an official contact address has been configured.
